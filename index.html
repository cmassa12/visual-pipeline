Operating model

Planning lives on a shared calendar and weekly sync. The DAM stores approved assets — it is not the briefing system.

Do this

  • Shared 90-day visual calendar (magazine + sprints)
  • Weekly sync: shipping, needs, gaps, outreach owners
  • Lead-time standards baked into planning
  • DAM as storage; optional Friday digest later

Stop doing this

  • Email after every assignment as the “system”
  • “Just check the DAM” with no brief
  • Same-week asks for planned magazine/sprint work
  • Buying Teams integration before briefing exists
Core problem. Without a forward calendar, photo can’t run manager → dean → faculty in time — so planned pages inherit whatever was shot that week, or generic screens.

Lead times & quality bar

Good campus photography needs outreach time. Same-week requests force weak fallbacks.

Work type Notice Why
Alumni magazine feature 8–12 weeks Dean → faculty → real lab/kitchen/clinic moment
Web / campaign sprint 3–6 weeks Schedule hands-on activity, not screens
Known campus event Per event calendar Still list coverage on the visual calendar
Breaking / reactive Same week Exception lane only — not the default

Prefer

  • Hands-on making, cooking, building, treating, practicing
  • Clear story beat — who is doing what, and why it matters
  • Specific programs and spaces named in the brief

Avoid as default

  • Students at laptops with no distinctive action
  • Vague “campus life” with no tie to the piece
  • Posing that could be anyone, anywhere

Sample dean outreach

We’re planning visuals for [piece] (need-by [date]). We’re looking for a hands-on moment in [program] — e.g. [lab demo / kitchen service / clinic / build critique] — not computer lab. Could you connect us with a faculty member who has something real happening in the next [X] weeks that we could photograph?

DAM & request policy

PhotoShelter stores approved assets. Discovery for planned work starts on the calendar, not in random gallery browsing.

Notifications

  • No routine email after every assignment
  • Optional Friday digest with gallery links is fine
  • Park paid Teams alerts until briefing cadence works
  • Skip a staging gallery while event galleries already exist

Student organizations

  • Never share full albums or DAM access
  • Curated set only after edit/approval
  • Only if the group is clearly identifiable
  • Include credit and usage notes

Student org reply

Thanks for reaching out. We don’t share full albums or DAM access, and Frost & Lights was covered as a general campus event — so we may not have dedicated shots of your group.

Once editing is done, I can check whether any approved images clearly feature your group. If we have a couple that work, I’m happy to send those for your pages with credit/usage notes. If not, we’ll let you know.

If you share where you plan to post them and roughly how many you need, that helps.

Director day-one handoff

Ten-minute walkthrough for the incoming design director. Hand them the model — then fill live themes together.

We want imagery that matches what’s actually shipping — magazine and sprints — not whatever happened to be shot that week. Good hands-on photos need weeks of dean/faculty coordination. A shared 90-day visual calendar plus a short weekly sync replaces “need this yesterday” and random DAM browsing.

First 30 days — ask of the director

  1. Own calendar visibility — need-bys and angles stay current
  2. Chair the weekly sync (or name a design lead)
  3. Share magazine + sprint pipeline as soon as known
  4. Partner on the hands-on quality bar
  5. Revisit paid Teams alerts only after briefing works

Success — first semester

  • Sprint pieces: visual needs listed ≥3 weeks out

  • Needs named on the calendar instead of “any new photos?”
